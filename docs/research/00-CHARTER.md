# Research — Charter

## Mission

Own research findings — competitive, user, market or technical insight — that inform Product Office decisions, so findings survive past the conversation in which they were surfaced.

## Authority

Findings inform decisions; they do not themselves authorize implementation. The Product Office decides how a finding is applied, and routes the resulting refinement to the owning office (`docs/OFFICES.md` § Refinement-ownership routing).

## Responsibilities

- Record research findings with their source and date as they are produced.
- Flag when a finding materially changes prior guidance in another office, so that office's documentation can be updated.

## Inputs

- Research requested by the Product Office.
- External sources cited at the time of recording.

## Outputs

- Findings entries (none recorded yet — this office starts empty and is populated as research is actually performed).

## Approval authority

Product Office (Silvester Odilu) decides how a finding changes direction.

## Implementation ownership

None directly — findings are routed to the owning office before any implementation occurs.

## Dependencies

`docs/OFFICES.md`.
